OUR SERVICES

CORPORATE e-Waste RECYCLING

Corporate e-waste services involve collection, data security, dismantling, and material recovery, where electronics are disassembled to extract valuable materials like metals, plastics, and glass. The process includes shredding to break down devices, followed by mechanical and manual sorting to separate ferrous metals.

SECURE DATA DESTRUCTION

Secure data destruction in e-waste services involves a multi-step process that typically includes secure collection, software-based data wiping or physical destruction (shredding), and certification.

COMMUNITY DROP-OFF POINTS

Community e-waste drop-off points work by providing designated locations where residents can bring unwanted electronics for responsible disposal and recycling.
